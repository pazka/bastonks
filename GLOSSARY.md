# bastonks — glossary

**Board**: the Trello board "bastonks" where Salomé Klein kept the work of her PhD; the single source of everything shown. Trello's own default tip cards (« Astuce Trello ») are not part of the work and are never shown.

**Pigeon**: the character the visitor plays; the only way to approach and consult items up close.

**Backlog list / To-do list**: lists on the Board holding work not yet done.

**Done list**: a list holding finished work, usually named after the week it was finished in. A list with no date that has been archived also counts as done.

**Archived**: a card or list hidden from the Board in Trello but kept. Archived items are part of the work and are shown. An archived card counts as done, whatever list it sits in.
_Avoid_: deleted

**Golden Peanut**: a pickup placed next to an Exceptional Card. Picking one up gives the Pigeon a Boost. The work can be fully explored without any.

**Exceptional Card**: a card picked by hand after reviewing the Board because it stands out: a milestone, a card with unusually rich content, or a personal favourite. Being exceptional is a curated choice, not something computed.

**Boost**: a lasting improvement to the Pigeon from Golden Peanuts: easier discovery and climbing higher in the tree with less effort.

**Tree**: the in-world form of one list on the Board that holds at least one Done card; its Branches are those cards. A Backlog or To-do list gets a Tree for its archived cards, while its other cards stand as Seed Pots in the Greenhouse.

**Found**: a Branch or Seed Pot the visitor has inspected up close with the Pigeon. Approaching it is not enough. Found items count toward the visitor's completion.

**Done card**: a card that sits in a Done list or has been archived.

**Branch**: the in-world form of one Done card, growing on its list's Tree. Its leaves take the colour of the season of its Tree's Week, unless the card has its own colour in Trello.

**Fruit**: the in-world form of a label on a card, hanging on that card's Branch. Each label is its own fruit species.

**Week**: the week a Tree stands for. It is read from its list's name, or from its cards' dates when the name has no date or a date that doesn't fit them.

**Path**: the winding way through the forest along which the done Trees stand in week order. Following it means walking through the work's timeline.

**Greenhouse**: the area apart from the forest where unfinished work grows.

**Seed Pot**: the in-world form of one card from a Backlog or To-do list that is not archived, standing in the Greenhouse. A card that is in progress (list « En cours ») is a Seed Pot with a visible sprout.

**New Growth**: Branches and Seed Pots that appeared since the visitor's last visit. They grow in visibly on arrival and stay highlighted.

**Flyover**: the opening camera glide over the whole forest, showing the thesis title, Salomé's name and an epigraph beneath them, before control passes to the Pigeon.

**Masked**: personal details (e-mail addresses, phone numbers, patient codes) replaced by a neutral marker before anything leaves the server. People's first names stay visible.
